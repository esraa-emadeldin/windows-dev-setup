# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people who are new
(to Windows, to PowerShell, or to open source), and keep feedback about the work,
not the person. Harassment of any kind is not accepted.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, please report it privately to the maintainer through
a [private security advisory](https://github.com/esraa-emadeldin/windows-dev-setup/security/advisories/new)
(it is private, even though it says "security"). Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that break this code.
